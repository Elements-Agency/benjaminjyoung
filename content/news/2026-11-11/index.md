### Stark County Artist's Exhibit

The Stark County Artists Exhibition is now officially open over at the Massillon Museum, featuring a diverse lineup of regional works—including my piece, I'm Sorry, It's Cancer. While it's always an interesting exercise to see how the annual jury selections shake out and which pieces capture the judges' particular sensibilities every year, the exhibition certainly offers plenty to ponder about the current local art landscape. Be sure to stop by and take in the gallery if you are in the area.

### ActLoCLE Exhibit

The 3rd Annual ActLoCLE exhibit has announced that my watercolor painting, "I'm Sorry, It's Cancer", has been awarded an Honorable Mention. ActLoCLE brings local artists from the Cleveland area, and this is my third straight year being selected to be a part of this exhibit. Both the exhibit and my painting career share their age of three years, so this exhibit holds a special place for me.

## New Painting: Fishing Bay

Sometimes a piece begins purely as a painter’s exercise—a quick response to a landscape, a sudden impulse of light or form. Lately, inspired by the Andrew Wyeth exhibit currently showing at the Cleveland Museum of Art and drawing from reference photos from my Delaware trips, I found myself drawn back to the water.

Fishing Bay came together naturally from those moments.
