## Exhibition Updates & Accolades

I am honored to share several recent exhibition acceptances and awards:

* **Stark County Artists Exhibit (2026):** My watercolor painting *"I'm Sorry, It's Cancer"* has been officially accepted into this year's exhibition, hosted at the **Massillon Museum**. 
* **Door 2 Art Show (2026):** *"I'm Sorry, It's Cancer"* has also been accepted into the 2026 Door 2 Art Show. This deeply personal piece holds a significant place in my current narrative series, and I am grateful to see it resonating with jurors and audiences alike.
* **Stark County Fair:** For the first time, I entered works into the Stark County Fair, walking away with **three blue 1st-place ribbons** across different painting categories, alongside **two red 2nd-place ribbons**.
* **Upcoming Exhibits:** I have several additional exhibition entries currently pending review—stay tuned for updates as those decisions are finalized.

## Studio Progress & Health Update

Transparency is important in my relationship with collectors, as my lived experiences directly inform the narrative realism of my work. 

My ongoing cancer treatment continues to be a demanding chapter. Managing multiple specialist appointments each week has been overwhelming at times. Current medical evaluations suggest the possibility that the cancer may have spread, and I am actively planning for a second surgery followed by radiation therapy. Additionally, because I am losing my voice frequently, doctors share a real concern that it may have impacted my vocal cords; I will be consulting with my ENT specialist very soon for further evaluation. 

Despite these health challenges, the work in the studio provides steady grounding. Progress on my **Dolly Parton egg tempera painting** continues forward, on schedule for the upcoming feature show at **Second April** this coming October. The traditional egg tempera medium demands immense patience and meticulous layering, and watching this portrait emerge has been a profound anchor during a rigorous season.
