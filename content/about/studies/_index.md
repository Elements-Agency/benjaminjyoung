At first glance, this site is a clean visual art portfolio. Beneath the surface, it’s run by a former software engineer and data scientist who approaches a canvas with the same dry, analytical rigor usually reserved for code reviews and lab experiments.

Art and data science aren't opposites—both are just systematic methods for observing the world, spotting hidden patterns, and desperately attempting to build order out of chaos.

## The Lab Notebook ##
