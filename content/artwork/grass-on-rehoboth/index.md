Captured during a quiet winter trip to the Delaware coast, **Grass on Rehoboth** explores the stark, sun-bleached beauty of the shoreline during the off-season. Stripped of the summer crowds, the coastal landscape reveals its underlying structure—wind-scoured dunes, dormant beach grass, and the crisp, low-angled light of a winter afternoon.

In the foreground, clusters of dry dune grass catch the bright coastal sun, casting long, rhythmic shadows across the pale, wind-rippled sand. Beyond the ridge of the dune, the Atlantic stretches out in deep, slate-blue tones, catching a subtle glint of winter light near the horizon beneath a cool, expansive sky.
