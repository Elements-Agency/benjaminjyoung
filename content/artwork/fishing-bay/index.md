A quiet study of coastal light and shifting weather along the Delaware coast. *Fishing Bay* captures the stillness of moored vessels resting on glassy, reflective waters beneath a heavy, atmospheric sky. 

## About the Work

Inspired by time spent sketching and photographing the working waterways of Delaware, this piece balances the raw, unpredictable movement of wet-in-wet sky washes against the crisp, deliberate silhouettes of the anchored boats and distant shoreline. 

* **Atmosphere & Light:** Heavy slate grays and muted ultramarine clouds break just enough to cast a warm, diffused glow across the water's surface, highlighting the subtle ripples and vertical reflections of the masts.
* **Composition & Palette:** Built around a restrained, earth-toned palette, the warm ochre hull of the central boat serves as a quiet focal point against the cool blues and neutral grays of the bay, while a flock of gulls cuts diagonally through the heavy air to guide the eye across the horizon.
* **Technique:** Executed in transparent watercolor on heavy cotton paper, relying on the untouched white of the paper to preserve the brightest breaks in the clouds and the sheen on the water.
