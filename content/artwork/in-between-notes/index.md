*In Between Notes* captures a quiet, unguarded pause beneath the stage lights. Rather than depicting the height of a performance, the composition focuses on the breath just before the next lyric—eyes lifted, expression reflective, and the gold microphone waiting just out of reach against a deep, velvety black void.

The lighting across her hair, ruffled blouse, and hoop earrings creates a sculptural glow against the dark background, emphasizing both the glamour of the stage and the vulnerability of a solitary moment in front of an audience.

## Process & Medium

This piece marks my first completed work in **egg tempera**—a traditional medium made by hand-grinding dry pigments with egg yolk and water. Created specifically for an invitational Dolly Parton tribute group exhibition, the painting relies on hundreds of fine, translucent layers and delicate cross-hatching to build luminous skin tones, soft curls, and subtle shifts in shadow. 

Because egg tempera dries almost instantaneously upon touching the panel, every brushstroke requires deliberate pacing—mirroring the quiet, measured stillness captured in the subject herself.
